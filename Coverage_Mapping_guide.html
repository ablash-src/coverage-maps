Coverage Mapping Guide & Definitions

How to Use the Map

  • Click and drag to pan the map
  • Scroll to zoom in/out
  • Click on markers to view store details
  • Use layer controls (top-right) to toggle coverage areas and partners
  • Hover over regions for demand data

About

This map visualizes the geographic coverage of our returns partner network, showing the relationship between customer demand and store locations.

Coverage is calculated using straight-line distance between customer zip code centroids and partner store zip codes, within specified distances (1–10, 15, 20, 25, 30, and 33 miles/km).

Data updates automatically from the coverage pipeline every Tuesday.

Metric Definitions

CoverageCustomer return volume within specified distance of a partner store
1P PartnersFirst-party retail partners (Amazon-owned locations)
3P PartnersThird-party retail partners (e.g., Kohl's, Staples)
Demand UnitsTotal number of returned units for the analysis period
Store MappingGeographic assignment of customer zip codes to nearest partner stores
Combined Coverage %(Combined Coverage Units / Demand Units) × 100
Overlap CoverageUnits covered by multiple partners within X miles of the same customer zip code
Units per Store/DayDaily units volume spread across stores: (Coverage Units) / (Store Count × 365)
Incr Coverage BPSBasis Points increase in Combined Coverage % introduced by a potential partner
