Guide & Definitions

How to Use

  • Click and drag to pan the map
  • Scroll to zoom in/out
  • Click on markers to view store details
  • Use layer controls to toggle coverage areas
  • Hover over regions for demand data

Definitions

Coverage Customer return volume within specified distance of a partner store
1P Partners First-party retail partners (Amazon-owned locations)
3P Partners Third-party retail partners (e.g., Kohl's, Staples)
Demand Units Total returned units for the analysis period
Store Mapping Geographic assignment of customer zip codes to nearest partner stores

About

This map visualizes the geographic coverage of our returns partner network, showing the relationship between customer demand and store locations.
